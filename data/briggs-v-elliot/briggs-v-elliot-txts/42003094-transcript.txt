THE CITIZENS COUNCIL PUTTING ON MEMBERSHIP DRIVE

The Executive Committee of the Clarendon Citizens Council has put on a membership campaign beginning Friday, November 29th, and continuing thru Tuesday, Dec. 10th. It is our plan to have a meeting of the Council Tuesday night December 10th to receive the report of the membership committee and discuss matters of vital interest. A detailed announcement of this meeting will be made at a later date.

The Executive Committee has appointed the membership committee as followers: Ernest K. Mathis, Chairman; George R. Jones, Alcolu; Ben J. Staton, Foreston; J. C. Phillips, Bloomville; P. C. Johnson, Wyboo and Jordan sections; Charlton McLeod, Paxville. These men will have receipt books at their homes or place of business. Please don't wait for these men to contact you, but contact the man in your community and join up, please. The membership fee is $2.00 for year beginning Oct. 1, 1957, ending Sept. 30, 1958.

I assure you that its a fine constructive organization working for the best interest of all our people of both races. We hope to get on this drive from 800 to 1000 members.

Don't stay on the outside and criticize, but join with us and help make the Citizens Council what it should be.

L. B. McCord, Chairman
Citizens Council
