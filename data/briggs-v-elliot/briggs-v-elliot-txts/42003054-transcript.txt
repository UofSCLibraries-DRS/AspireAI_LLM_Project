November 24, 1951

Mr. L. B. McCord
County Superintendent of Education
Manning, South Carolina

Dear Sir:

The South Carolina Conference of National Association for the Advancement of Colored People is not only concerned but interested in supporting the Negro patrons of School district #2 who have appealed to you and the Board of Trustees of said district for a humane share of school funds for their children. The schools for Negroes in the Manning District fall far short of those for whites and even under the majority decision in the Clarendon County Case they should and must be equal. We are willing to support the Negro patrons of school district #2 in their fight for equal educational facilities.

We will appreciate your attention to their petition and our letter and request a reply to both.

Very truly yours,
James M. Hinton
President
