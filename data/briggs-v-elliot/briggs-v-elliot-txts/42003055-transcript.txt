October 9, 1957

Miss Frances Reedy
Converse College
Spartanburg, S. C.

Dear Miss Frances:

Yours received a few days ago and as I have been rather busy for the past few days, I'm just getting around to answer. Please excuse the delay.

With reference to teacher pay, may I say that all teachers white and negro get equal state aid pay based on their certificate as to group, class, grade, and the number of years experienced.

Each district may or may not supplement this state aid.

Our district here in Manning supplements on the following basis: Those holding a grade A certificate receives $45.00 supplement. Those holding a high B receives $35.00 supplement. Those holding a grade C to low B receives $10.00 supplement. There is no distinction so far as color goes. There is no law or rule on the state level what the supplement shall be if any.

In Clarendon County we have 2528 white enrolled and 6486 negros enrolled. All have equal facilities. There is no distinction whatever.

We have now and intend to continue our segregated schools.

Segregation of races in morally and spiritually right. God made both the white and the negro. I don't know why and have sense enough not to question His acts. I am sure He expects us to remain as he created us.

Now, who created the mulatto or mixed breed is the 64 thousand dollar question. I am sure of one thing God had no part in it. Segregation was practiced in the Old Testament times.

Mans conduct is not perfect since something happened to him in the Garden.
