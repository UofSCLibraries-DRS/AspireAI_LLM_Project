Route 5 Box
Manning, South Carolina
October 1, 1947


Mr. Vander Stukes, Chairman
Board of Trustees, School District #26
Clarendon County
Davis Station, South Carolina

Dear Sir:

     On 28 July 1947, a Petition on behalf of my children and all other school children in School District #26, Clarendon County, South Carolina, was mailed to you, since that date I have had no communication from you with regard to this petition nor has any efforts been made to furnish bus transportation to Negroes in this School District.

     Please advise me if efforts are being made to furnish school bus transportation.

Very truly yours,
Levi Pearson
Levi Pearson

Mr Levi Pearson
manning SC

Dear Levi replying to yours of 1st Inst.
The trustees of Davis Station school district will hear your complaint on October 10th Friday - at 5 30 P.M.
Please be present with all
Yours very truly EG Stukes Chairman
Davis Station School board of trustees
