On the other hand the birds of the air, the fish of the sea, and the animals of the field were created by God and he endowed them with instinct which is as perfect today as when He created them. They all practiced segregation and did not mix.

So you see your professor as a spirit (a soul) endowed with intellect, sensibility and will which are attributes of the spirit are not so perfect as the instinct of birds, beast and fish. Thus he misjudges what God's will is in the matter. You see man fell in the garden and became a sinner. So the attributes of a spiritual being were twisted and warped having now a soul full of iniquity.

Segregation is morally and spiritually right and is in the highest interest of society.
