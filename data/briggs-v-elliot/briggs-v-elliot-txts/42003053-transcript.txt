MANNING, SOUTH CAROLINA    NOVEMBER 8, 1951


From:    The Negro Patrons' Committee
         School District Number Two
         Clarendon County, South Carolina

To:      Trustee Board of Schools
         School District Number Two
         Clarendon County, South Carolina
         Manning, South Carolina

To:      Clarendon County Board of School Trustees

To:      Clarendon County Delegation

To:      State Officials of Education


Gentlemen:

         We, the Colored people of School District Two, Clarendon County,
South Carolina, appeal to you, our representatives, for a humane share of
distribution for educational purposes for our children.

         If we had members of our race on the District Board of Education,
the County Board of Education, the State Board of Education, and on our
County Delegation, this type of appeal would not be necessary. But, since
we are taxed without representation; since we are governed without a voice
in our government, we have to use this method of appeal (begging) for what
we know we are entitled to.

         We have heard that schools are going to be built for Negroes. No
one has called in a single Negro and told him anything at all about the
schools. Some of us read the newspapers.

         Between the time when the schools are to be built and the present,
we want to exist a little better. Therefore, we request your immediate con-
sideration as follows:
