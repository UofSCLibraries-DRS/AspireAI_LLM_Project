LONG DAY LETTER GV2, COLUMBIA, SOUTH CAROLINA, MAY 17, 1954
HONORABLE JAMES R. COKER, HARTSVILLE, SOUTH CAROLINA
IN ACCORDANCE WITH YOUR REQUEST I QUOTE STATEMENT ISSUED TODAY
BY GOVERNOR BYRNES: " TIME AND AGAIN SUPREME COURT HAS HELD THE
DOCTRINE OF SEPARATE BUT EQUAL SCHOOL FACILITIES NOT VIOLATIVE
OF THE CONSTITUTION. AM SHOCKED TO LEARN COURT HAS REVERSED
ITSELF. I WILL MAKE NO FURTHER COMMENT UNTIL I HAVE HAD AN
OPPORTUNITY TO READ AND STUDY IT. NEWS REPORTS INDICATE NO DECREE
HAS BEEN HANDED DOWN BY THE COURT IN CLARENDON CASE THAT WOULD
IMMEDIATELY FORCE APPROXIMATELY 295 WHITE CHILDREN OF THAT SCHOOL
DISTRICT TO ATTEND THE NEGRO SCHOOLS WITH APPROXIMATELY 2,799
NEGRO CHILDREN. THE FIRST REPORTS INDICATE COURT WILL FIRST HEAR
ARGUMENT AT OCTOBER TERM AS TO THE LANGUAGE OF THE DECREE. WE
MUST AWAIT FINAL DECREE THE SUPREME COURT AND I EARNESTLY URGE
ALL OF OUR PEOPLE, WHITE AND COLORED TO EXERCISE RESTRAINT AND
PRESERVE ORDER. I HAVE ALWAYS STATED THAT WHAT COURSE SOUTH
CAROLINA WOULD FOLLOW IN CASE OF AN ADVERSE DECISION WAS A
QUESTION WHICH ONLY THE LEGISLATURE COULD DECIDE. WHAT
RECOMMENDATION I WOULD MAKE TO THE LEGISLATURE I WOULD DECIDE
ONLY AFTER I HAVE HAD OPPORTUNITY TO STUDY THE DECREE OF SUPREME
COURT. THIS AFTERNOON I WILL CONFER WITH SENATOR GRESSETTE,
CHAIRMAN OF THE COMMITTEE APPOINTED THREE YEARS AGO TO STUDY
THIS QUESTION, WHICH COMMITTEE HERETOFORE AT MY REQUEST HAS HELD
NO PUBLIC HEARINGS" SIGNED MISS CASSIE CONNOR, SECRETARY
