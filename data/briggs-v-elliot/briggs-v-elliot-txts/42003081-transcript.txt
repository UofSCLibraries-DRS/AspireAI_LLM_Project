THE ASSOCIATION OF CITIZENS' COUNCILS
OF SOUTH CAROLINA

Olanta, South Carolina

March 1, 1958


Dear Mr. Chairman:

     I have been recently appointed by The Association of 
Citizens' Councils of South Carolina as Chairman of a move- 
ment to organize the ministers in South Carolina who believe 
in segregation of the races.

     I will appreciate your cooperation in sending, to us, 
the names and addresses, on the enclosed questionnaire, of 
ministers who are members of the Citizens' Council in your 
area, also ministers who are not members.

     It is my honest opinion that there are many of our mini-
sters who will rally to an Organization such as we are proposing 
if they are contacted. Our Organization will function within 
the framework of The Association of Citizens' Councils of South 
Carolina; but will hold separate meetings to carry out our part 
of the work. Ministers will be notified of meetings in the 
future.

     Your prayerful cooperation will be appreciated.

                    Sincerely yours,

                    M. A. Woodson
                    M. A. Woodson, Pastor
                    Bethel Baptist Church
                    Olanta, South Carolina
