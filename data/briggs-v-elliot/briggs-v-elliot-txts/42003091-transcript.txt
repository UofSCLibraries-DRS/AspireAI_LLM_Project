December 7, 1957

Mr. W. O. McElveen
Olanta, S. C.

Dear Mr. McElveen:

Yours just received asking me to address your Citizens Council in Olanta. I shall be very glad to do so, either on December 16 or 17, whichever suits your council best.

Please advise me to date, hour, and place.

Sincerely yours,

L. B. McCord
Supt. of Education
Clarendon County
