Hampton Inst.
Hampton, Va.
March 7 - 06.

Mr. J. S. Levy,
Camden, S. C.

Dear Father,

My privilege of being a Hampton student and of living this strenuous life for the past four years have in some ways revolutionized my whole life.

One of the best things for a young man who desires to improve himself is to have a modle to go by. It is true that we have Christ for an example, but it means

=== Page 2 ===
so much more to young men when they can see this life and spirit in the lives of those who are working side by side with them and laboring under the same difficulties.
Such has been my opportunity at Hampton. I cannot attend a single meeting, walk the streets about our grounds, or visit a single student's room without seeing or hearing something that reminds me of the fact that there is still a chance for me to improve my life.
It will not be long now before this glorious life shall end here, but I hope to take the precepts and examples with me wherever duty calls me.
Hoping to hear from you again soon I am your affectionate son,
Isaac.
