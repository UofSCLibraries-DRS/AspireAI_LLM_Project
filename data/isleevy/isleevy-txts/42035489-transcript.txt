Dear Mr. Johnson:

I enclosed the history of my father, also a letter he wrote while in school, news articles and his obituary. You did not say whether you wanted pictures?

Doctor Leevy lives at 907 C Avenue, Bayonne, New Jersey, you might be interested in him also he is a great writer, speaker and teacher traveling over the world. He was born in Columbia. I enclosed one of his latest writings.

The State and Record library informed me that Mr. Sam Latimer Extension 231 State Record Company Stadium Road may help give some informative information on him and the newspapers years before the library started.

Thanking you very much.

Your friend,
Ruby Leevy Johnson
(Mrs.) Ruby Leevy Johnson.

P. S. Former House of Representatives I. S. Leevy Johnson (Attorney Johnson) Post Office Box 11185 is a grandson.
